Connect Buffer

What you schedule in Buffer also lands in Telegram and Discord, at the same minute.

Relaypost watches your Buffer queue. When Buffer publishes a post, the same text, image and link go to your Telegram channel and your Discord server a few seconds later. No copy and paste.

Connect Buffer

Built on the Buffer API. Relaypost reads your scheduled posts. The only thing it writes is a tag on a post, when you turn a destination on for it.
